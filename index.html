Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We're headquartered at Old Toll Gate, Ikosi-Ketu, Lagos, and deliver across Ketu, Ikosi, Mile 12, Ojota, Alapere, Magodo, Ikeja, Surulere, Victoria Island, plus Mowe, Ibafo, Magboro, Arepo, Berger in Ogun, and Sagamu and Abeokuta. Message us on WhatsApp if your area isn't listed — we're always expanding.

How do I place an order?

Simply send your shopping list to us on WhatsApp at +234 913 118 5150, or use the order form on this page — it opens WhatsApp with your order ready to send. We confirm availability and price, you pay by bank transfer, and we deliver.

What are your delivery times and fees?

Orders confirmed by 2pm are delivered the same day (by 6pm). Orders after 2pm arrive the next day. Delivery fees range from ₦2,000 to ₦10,000 depending on location and order size — and delivery is free on orders above ₦50,000.

What if an item on my list is unavailable?

We'll let you know immediately on WhatsApp and suggest the closest alternative or remove it from your order — you only pay for what we deliver.

Can I order prescription medicines?

Yes. We work with licensed pharmacists and follow PCN regulations. Send us a photo of your prescription on WhatsApp and we'll handle the rest with the utmost care and discretion.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer, confirmed before we dispatch your order. Simple, safe, and trackable.
