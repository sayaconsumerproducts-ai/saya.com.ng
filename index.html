Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We're headquartered at Old Toll Gate, Ikosi-Ketu, Lagos, and deliver across Ketu, Ikosi, Mile 12, Ojota, Alapere, Magodo, Ikeja, Surulere, Victoria Island, plus Mowe, Ibafo, Magboro, Arepo, Berger in Ogun, and Sagamu and Abeokuta. Message us if your area isn't listed.

How do I place an order?

Send your shopping list on WhatsApp at +234 901 665 1148, or use the form on this page. We confirm availability and price, you pay by bank transfer, and we deliver.

What are your delivery times and fees?

Orders confirmed by 2pm are delivered the same day (by 6pm). Orders after 2pm arrive the next day. Delivery fees from ₦2,000 to ₦10,000 — free on orders above ₦50,000.

What if an item is unavailable?

We'll let you know immediately and suggest the closest alternative — you only pay for what we deliver.

Can I order prescription medicines?

Yes. We work with licensed pharmacists and follow PCN regulations. Send a photo of your prescription on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer, confirmed before we dispatch your order.
